Hey, I'm Saba.

I'm a physiotherapy graduate from Jamia Hamdard, New Delhi, with a particular interest in orthopedic and musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

My clinical training has involved working across government hospitals and a medical college, where I worked with patients experiencing low back pain, post-surgical knee and shoulder conditions, and other musculoskeletal disorders. These experiences taught me the importance of careful assessment and movement analysis in rehabilitation.

Alongside clinical practice, I have been involved in research on subjective cognitive decline, sleep quality, and daily functioning in middle-aged adults. I am particularly interested in the intersection of rehabilitation and artificial intelligence, including the evaluation of AI-generated exercise rehabilitation content and the broader role of digital health technologies in physiotherapy.

I am always happy to connect and collaborate on projects related to rehabilitation, digital health, and AI in healthcare. Feel free to reach out at sabashahin021 [at] gmail [dot] com.
